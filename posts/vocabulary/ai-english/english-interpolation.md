### **interpolation** (noun)

**Basic meaning:**
*Interpolation* is the process of **adding something between existing parts**, often to fill in missing information or estimate values between known points.

The word is used in both **everyday language (slightly informal sense)** and **technical fields (math, data, computer science).**

---

## 1. Everyday meaning (general English)

In normal speech, *interpolation* means:
> inserting words, ideas, or information into something that already exists.

### Example:
- “The editor made a few interpolations in the original text to clarify the meaning.”
- “I added an interpolation in the story to explain why the character left.”

👉 Think of it as: **“filling in the gaps” or “adding something in between.”**

---

## 2. Mathematics / data science meaning (most common technical use)

In math, *interpolation* means:
> estimating unknown values that lie **between known data points**.

### Simple example:
You know:
- At 10°C, ice cream sales = 100
- At 20°C, ice cream sales = 200

You want to estimate sales at 15°C → that’s **interpolation**.

So you might estimate:
- At 15°C → ~150 sales

👉 You are **predicting values between known points**.

---

## 3. Computer science / programming usage

In programming, interpolation often refers to:
> inserting variables or expressions into strings.

### Example (JavaScript):
```javascript
let name = "Alice";
console.log(`Hello, ${name}!`);
```

That process is called **string interpolation**.

### Example (R - related to your interests):
```r
name <- "Alice"
glue::glue("Hello, {name}!")
```

This is **string interpolation in R**.

---

## 4. Simple mental picture

Think of a graph with two points:

- Point A
- Point B

Interpolation is like:
> drawing a smooth line between them and guessing what happens in the middle.

Or in writing:
> inserting a sentence between two existing sentences to make the meaning clearer.

---

## 5. Common daily-life phrasing

You might hear:
- “That’s just an interpolation between the data points.”
- “The software uses interpolation to estimate missing values.”
- “He interpolated a comment into the conversation.”

---

## 6. Quick summary

- **Core idea:** putting something *in between*
- **In language:** adding extra explanation
- **In math/data:** estimating between known values
- **In coding:** inserting variables into text
