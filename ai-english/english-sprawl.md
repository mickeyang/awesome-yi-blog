## 🔤 Pronunciation
**sprawl** /sprɔːl/ (sounds like *spr-* + *all*, with a long "aw" vowel)

---

## 📖 Meaning

**Sprawl** can be a **verb** or a **noun**.

### 1. Verb — "to spread out lazily"
When a person or animal **sprawls**, they sit or lie with arms and legs stretched out in a relaxed / messy way.

- He **sprawled** on the couch watching TV.
- The cat **sprawled** across my keyboard. 😼

### 2. Verb — "to grow outwards in an uncontrolled way"
Used for **cities/towns** — when buildings spread out messily instead of staying compact.

- The city **sprawls** for miles into the suburbs.
- LA is famous for its **urban sprawl**.

### 3. Noun — "the state of sprawling"
- *Urban sprawl* → 城市无序扩张
- *The dog lay in a sprawl on the floor.*

---

## 🧩 Grammar tip
As a verb, **sprawl** is usually **intransitive** (no direct object), and often pairs with **on / across / into**:

- sprawl **on** the bed
- sprawl **across** the seat
- sprawl **into** the countryside
