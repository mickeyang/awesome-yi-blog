**Tiny concept**

Cryptic is an adjective. It describes something (a message, a comment, a clue, even an animal's coloring) that is hard to understand because it hides its real meaning on purpose.

The word comes from Greek *kryptos* = "hidden". That same root gives us **crypt** (the underground room in a church), **cryptography** (writing in code), and even the element name **krypton** (yes, Superman's planet Krypton shares the root too).

Important nuance: *cryptic* is not just "hard to understand" — it specifically suggests there IS a meaning, but it's concealed. Compare:
- A physics textbook is *difficult*, but not necessarily *cryptic*.
- A text from your ex saying "we need to talk about the thing" — that's *cryptic*.
