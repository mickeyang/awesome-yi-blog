### 📚 Meaning & Usage
**Hunch** has two main uses:

#### 1. **Noun: A guess based on intuition (not facts)**  
👉 *Synonym: gut feeling*
- *I have a **hunch** that it’s going to rain today.*
- *She trusted her **hunch** and bought the cheaper stock—it doubled in value!*

#### 2. **Verb: To bend your body forward, often because you’re cold, shy, or hiding**
- *He **hunched** his shoulders against the wind.*
- *Don’t **hunch** over your phone—it’s bad for your neck.*

⚠️ **Common mistake**: Don’t confuse **hunch** with **hint**.
- ❌ *I have a hint it will rain.* → Incorrect for intuition.
- ✅ *I have a **hunch** it will rain.* → Correct.
- ✅ *She gave me a **hint** about the surprise party.* → Correct (a clue).
