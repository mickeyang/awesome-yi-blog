## 🔤 Pronunciation
**er·go·nom·ics** → /ˌɜːrɡəˈnɑːmɪks/ (ur-guh-**NOM**-iks)

Stress is on the *third* syllable: **NOM**. The "er" at the start sounds like the start of *early*.

---

## 📖 What it means
**Ergonomics** = the study of designing things (chairs, desks, keyboards, tools) so they fit the human body comfortably and efficiently. Goal: reduce strain / injury and make work easier.

- Comes from Greek: *ergon* (work) + *nomos* (laws) → literally "the laws of work."
- The adjective form is **ergonomic** (e.g. *an ergonomic chair*).

---

## 💬 Examples in context
1. *I bought an **ergonomic** keyboard because my wrists were hurting.*
2. *This office was designed with **ergonomics** in mind — adjustable desks, good lighting, lumbar support.*
3. *Bad **ergonomics** can lead to back pain and carpal tunnel.*

You'll see it a lot in office/product marketing and occupational health.
