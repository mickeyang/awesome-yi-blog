### 1. Pronunciation & Meaning
*   **Pronunciation:** /ɪˈfem.ər.əl/ (ih-FEM-uh-rul)
*   **Meaning:** Lasting for a very short time. Think of soap bubbles, a shooting star, or a trend on social media.
*   **Level-up Synonym:** *Fleeting* or *transient*.

### 2. Tiny Concept: The "Life Cycle" of the Word
It comes from the Greek word *ephemeros*, meaning "lasting only a day." This is why mayflies (insects that live for just 24 hours) are called "ephemerids."

**Grammar Note:** It’s almost always used as an **adjective**.
*   *The ephemeral nature of youth.*
*   *An ephemeral trend.*

**Adverb form:** **Ephemerally**
*   *The artist ephemerally painted on the sand, knowing the tide would wash it away.*

### 3. Context Examples
*   **Nature:** "Cherry blossoms are beautiful because they are **ephemeral**; they fall after just a week."
*   **Tech/Social Media:** "Internet fame is often **ephemeral**—here today, gone tomorrow."
*   **Emotions:** "He felt an **ephemeral** moment of joy before the stress returned."
